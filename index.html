Why AWT

Building a device is hard. We make it simpler.

Most companies with an idea for a device run into the same problems. Here is how we solve them.

“I have an idea, but no engineering team.”

We take it from a rough description to a working device. You don't need a technical specification — we help you write one.

“I'm juggling designers, factories and IT suppliers.”

Design, development, testing, production and support all come from one partner. One contact person, no finger-pointing between vendors.

“I'm worried faulty units will reach my customers.”

Every unit is checked before it leaves, and the result is recorded — so you know exactly what you are shipping.

“Fixing a bug means recalling devices.”

With remote updates you can fix and improve devices that are already with your customers — safely, without site visits.

“I don't know how my devices are doing once they're sold.”

Live data and alerts let you spot a problem before a customer calls about it.

“I don't want to be locked in to one supplier.”

All designs and code are handed over to you. You are free to take them anywhere, at any time.

Services

What we can do for you.

Use us for the whole journey or just the step you need. Every service works on its own, and together they take your product from idea to devices in your customers' hands. Because one team covers every engineering area, the electronics, software and online services fit together from the start.

01

Design and development

Turn your idea into a working device.

We design the electronics and the software that runs inside them, and build prototypes you can test, show to investors or put in front of customers. Already have a team? We can join them for the difficult parts.

  • From a rough idea to a working prototype
  • Electronics design
  • Software inside the device
  • Connection to phones, apps and the internet
  • Support for your existing team

02

Making the parts work together

Every part of your device working as one.

A device is built from many parts — sensors, screens, wireless modules. We make sure they all work together smoothly, either by building the solution for you or by using a proven ready-made one, whichever saves you more time and money.

Built for you

Made to fit your hardware exactly, and the code is fully yours.

Ready-made and proven

We pick, buy and integrate an existing solution — faster and cheaper.

  • Sensors, screens, wireless and more
  • Clear documentation included
  • Tested on your actual device

03

Testing

Catch faults before your customers do.

Whether we made your electronics or another factory did, we check every unit on test equipment built for your product. Each one gets a recorded pass or fail, so you know exactly what you are shipping.

  • Every unit checked
  • Test equipment made for your product
  • Electronics from any manufacturer
  • A recorded result for every unit
  • Durability testing

04

Production

From the first prototype to a full production batch.

Small and medium batches: we buy the parts, put the electronics together, install the software and test everything. You receive finished units, ready to go into your product.

  • We source all components
  • Assembly and software installation
  • Small and medium batches
  • Delivered ready to use

05

Remote updates

Keep improving devices after they ship.

Fix bugs and add features to devices already in your customers' hands — no recalls, no site visits. Updates are secure, go out to a few devices first, and are undone automatically if something goes wrong. We run the whole system for you.

  • No recalls or site visits
  • Secure updates
  • Rolled out step by step
  • Automatic undo if an update fails
  • Run and maintained by us

06

Device monitoring

Know how your devices are doing.

See how all your devices are doing in one place, with alerts when something needs attention. Clear overviews can be set up for your team, your service staff or your own customers.

  • All devices in one view
  • Alerts before customers notice
  • Overviews for your team or customers
  • Run and maintained by us

FAQ

Common questions.

I only have an idea. Is that enough?

Yes. Many projects start with a sketch or a short description. We help turn it into clear requirements and a plan before anything is built.

Can you help with a project that is already underway?

Yes. We can support your team on specific tasks, or take on the remaining work.

Who owns the result?

You do. All code, designs and documents are handed over to you.

How is the price decided?

After the first call you receive a written proposal: a fixed price for each stage, or billing by time spent if the scope is still open.

What happens after the product launches?

We can run the update and monitoring systems for you and keep improving your device's software.
