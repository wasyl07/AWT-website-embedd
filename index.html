Services

Engineering, manufacturing and operations for embedded products.

Each service can be contracted on its own, or combined to take a product from concept to a maintained fleet in the field.

01

Embedded development

Firmware for microcontrollers and embedded Linux, electronics design and board bring-up. Complete product development from concept to validated prototype, or specialist support for an existing engineering team.

  • Firmware in C, C++ and Rust
  • Zephyr, FreeRTOS, bare-metal
  • Schematic and PCB design
  • Board bring-up
  • BLE, CAN and wired interfaces
  • Embedded Linux (Buildroot)

02

Device drivers

Drivers for sensors, displays, radios, memories and other peripherals — written to order for your hardware and platform, or sourced from an existing commercial or open-source driver library and integrated into your project. Either way, you receive documentation, an example application and tests run on real hardware.

Custom development

A new driver for your chip, bus and RTOS, with full source code ownership.

Sourced from a library

We select, purchase and integrate a proven ready-made driver — shorter lead time and lower cost.

  • Sensors, displays, memories, radios
  • I²C, SPI, UART, CAN, USB
  • Zephyr, FreeRTOS, bare-metal, Linux
  • Documentation and example code
  • Tested on real hardware

03

Assembly and production runs

Small and medium series manufacturing: component sourcing, PCB assembly, programming and testing. Units are delivered finished, verified and ready for final integration.

  • Component sourcing
  • PCB assembly
  • Prototype to series
  • Programmed, tested units

04

Equipment and production testing

Testing of post-production boards — whether manufactured by AWT or by your contract manufacturer. Custom fixtures and test software flash each unit, exercise its I/O and interfaces and record a pass/fail result.

  • Post-production board testing
  • Pogo-pin test fixtures
  • Automated test software
  • End-of-line programming
  • Validation and stress testing

05

Hosted update server (OTA)

Firmware delivery infrastructure for devices in the field, together with the secure bootloader on the device. Releases are signed, rolled out in stages and automatically reverted if an update fails.

  • Signed images (MCUboot)
  • Staged rollouts
  • Automatic rollback
  • Hosting and maintenance

06

Telemetry hosting

Collection, storage and visualisation of fleet data: sensor readings, device health, logs and alerts. Dashboards for your engineering team, service staff or end customers.

  • Ingestion over MQTT / HTTP
  • Time-series storage
  • Grafana dashboards
  • Alerting
